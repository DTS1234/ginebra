# Es joc de ginebra — primary source

Transcription and English translation of the only written rules we have for the game.

**Source:** Juan Monjo Soliveres, *«Es joc de ginebra»*, pp. 32–35 of a local Tàrbena
publication (photographed pages, 4 images, supplied 2026-08-25).

**Language note.** The text is Catalan in the variety spoken in **Tàrbena** (Marina Baixa,
Alicante). It uses the *salat* article — `es`, `sa`, `ses`, `des` instead of `el`, `la`,
`les`, `del` — which is the Balearic feature Tàrbena keeps because the village was
repopulated from Mallorca in the 17th century. That is why the rules read as "Mallorcan".
The author states plainly that the game is *"un joc autòcton de Tàrbena"* — native to
Tàrbena — so the source is a village tradition, not a Balearic one.

**Status of this document.** It is the primary source. Where `spec.md` and this document
disagree, this document wins unless someone with the game in their hands says otherwise.
Passages that were obscured in the photographs, or that the source itself leaves
ambiguous, are marked **[uncertain]** and must not be implemented without confirmation.

---

## 1. Introducció — Introduction

> Es joc de ginebra és un joc autòcton de Tàrbena, molt popular i conegut per molts de
> tarbeners. Podríem dir que és un des nostres senyals d'identitat. […]
>
> Aquest escrit no pretén ensenyar a jugar a ginebra, perquè, per explicar es joc caldria
> escriure un llibre de mil planes (i encara es quedaria curt, per sa quantitat i varietat
> de jugades que es poden presentar). […]
>
> Aquest escrit només és un repàs de ses normes bàsiques i elementals des joc. Interpretar
> aquestes normes, això ja és cosa de cadegú.
>
> Sa ginebra es juga amb una baralla de quaranta cartes espanyola. Sa partida la componen
> cinc jugadors. A cada jugador li tocaran huit cartes. **Ses cartes es repartixen de
> quatre en quatre.**

**Translation.** Ginebra is a game native to Tàrbena, very popular and known to many
Tarbeners — one of our marks of identity. This piece does not set out to teach the game;
explaining it would take a thousand-page book and still fall short, given the number and
variety of plays that can come up. It is only a review of the basic and elementary rules
of the game — interpreting those rules is each player's own business.

Ginebra is played with a 40-card Spanish deck. A game is made up of **five players**. Each
player gets **eight cards**. **The cards are dealt four at a time.**

## 2. Trumfos — Trumps

> «L'espadilla» (as d'espases), és es principal trumfo.
> «La manilla», és es segon trumfo en importància. 7 d'oros, 7 de copes, 2 d'espases i 2 de bastos
> «El basto» (as de bastos), és es tercer trumfo en importància.
>
> Aquestos són es trumfos de cada pal, de major a menor importància:
>
> - **OROS:** Espadilla, Manilla (7), Basto, Rovell (as), Rei, Cavall, Sota, el 2, el 3, el 4 el 5 i el 6
> - **COPES:** Espadilla, Manilla (7), Basto, Carabassa (as), Rei, Cavall, Sota, el 2, el 3, el 4 el 5 i el 6
> - **ESPASES:** Espadilla, Manilla (2), Basto, Rei, Cavall, Sota, el 7, el 6, el 5 el 4 i el 3
> - **BASTOS:** Espadilla, Manilla (2), Basto, Rei, Cavall, Sota, el 7, el 6, el 5 el 4 i el 3
>
> Es diu «dengue» o que un jugador «té el dengue» quan té l'espadilla i el basto en es
> mateix temps.
>
> Es diu «estutxe» o que un jugador «té l'estutxe» quan té l'espadilla, la manilla i el
> basto en es mateix temps.

Margin notes:

> «L'espadilla», és es principal trumfo. Fora des joc, es diu que «fa una espadilla» ses
> persones molt espavilades.
>
> Exemples de ses paraules especials des joc de ginebra: «el rovell» i «la carabassa». Una
> cosa curiosa d'aquest joc és que per jugar es parla un poc «de lao», per exemple, en es
> articles o en es verbs: «fallar», «refallar», «trumfo»…

**Translation.** The **espadilla** (ace of swords) is the chief trump. The **manilla** is
the second trump in importance: the 7 of coins, the 7 of cups, the 2 of swords and the 2
of clubs. The **basto** (ace of clubs) is the third trump in importance.

These are the trumps of each suit, from greatest to least importance:

| Trump suit | Order, highest first |
|---|---|
| **Oros** | Espadilla, Manilla (7 of oros), Basto, Rovell (ace of oros), King, Horse, Sota, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 |
| **Copes** | Espadilla, Manilla (7 of copes), Basto, Carabassa (ace of copes), King, Horse, Sota, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 |
| **Espases** | Espadilla, Manilla (2 of espases), Basto, King, Horse, Sota, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3 |
| **Bastos** | Espadilla, Manilla (2 of bastos), Basto, King, Horse, Sota, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3 |

A player **has the *dengue*** when they hold the espadilla and the basto at the same time.
A player **has the *estutxe*** when they hold the espadilla, the manilla and the basto at
the same time.

Note the two suit-specific names for the ace: **rovell** is the ace of *oros*,
**carabassa** is the ace of *copes*.

**What this list does and does not settle.** It gives the order *within the trump suit*.
The source never states the order within a non-trump suit, so the position of the ace in a
plain suit is **[uncertain]** — see finding D-6 in `rules-diff.md`.

## 3. El posso — The pot

> Abans de començar a jugar es forma «el posso», que és sa quantitat o quota que aporta
> cada jugador a parts iguals i que es posa enmig de sa taula.
>
> El posso pot mudar segons sa quantidat que vulguen apostar es jugadors de comú acord.
>
> Si el posso s'acaba i es jugadors volen continuar jugant es torna a renovar o a afegir
> amb una quantidat a convenir i a parts iguals.
>
> Pel contrari, si sa partida s'allarga massa i el posso és encara molt gran, es pot
> repartir entre es jugadors, també a parts iguals.

**Translation.** Before play begins the ***posso*** is formed: the amount or stake each
player contributes **in equal parts**, placed in the middle of the table.

The posso can change according to how much the players agree in common to stake.

If the posso runs out and the players want to keep playing, it is renewed or topped up
with an amount to be agreed, again in equal parts.

Conversely, if the session drags on too long and the posso is still very large, it can be
**divided among the players, also in equal parts.**

> **This is the settlement model of the whole game.** Players do not pay each other. They
> *collect from* (`cobrar`) and *pay into* (`pagar`) the pot. The two sides of a settlement
> need not balance, and in the source's own tables they frequently do not.

## 4. Normes des joc — Rules of play

### 4.1 Who makes trumps

> No cal sortejar per vore qui fa primer. A sa primera mà farà trumfos qui tinga
> l'espadilla. Després farà trumfos qui siga mà o qui vaja a soles, encara que no siga mà.
> Guanya sa partida es qui va i fa 5 bases.

**Translation.** There is no need to draw lots to see who goes first. **On the first hand,
whoever holds the espadilla makes trumps.** After that, **whoever is *mà* makes trumps —
or whoever goes alone, even if they are not *mà*.** The hand is won by the side that
*goes* and makes **5 basas**.

### 4.2 Going, and being helped

> Es qui és mà fa trumfos des pal que més en té o que li convé. Si ell creu que amb ses
> seues cartes pot fer 5 bases, pot anar a soles i jugar ell contra es altres quatre. Si
> veu que ell no pot fer a soles ses 5 bases, cosa normal, haurà d'esperar que un altre
> jugador li posa el rei i serà aquest jugador es qui li aidarà i entre es dos intentaran
> de fer ses 5 bases.

**Translation.** The *mà* makes trumps in the suit they hold most of, or that suits them.
If they believe their cards can make 5 basas, they may **go alone** (*anar a soles*) and
play one against the other four. If they see they cannot make the 5 basas alone — the
normal case — they must **wait for another player to put the king on them**; that player
is the one who will help them, and between the two of them they will try to make the 5
basas.

### 4.3 Putting the king

> Normalment sempre sol posar el rei es qui té bones cartes. Això es diu «posar el rei». Un
> jugador que no té rei no el pot posar. Moltes voltes poses el rei sense voler perquè et
> cau.
>
> Si es qui és mà li cau el rei s'acaba sa mà. Si et cau el rei, te l'has de jugar
> forçosament.
>
> Si es qui és mà té bones cartes però veu que té un «rei pelat» que li pot caure, pot dir:
> «es primer rei aida», i aixina es altres jugadors li'l posaran.

**Translation.** Normally it is the player with good cards who puts the king. This is
called ***posar el rei***. A player with no king cannot put one. **Many times you put the
king without meaning to, because it falls on you** (you are forced to play it).

**If the *mà*'s own king falls, the hand ends.** If your king falls, you are obliged to
play it.

If the *mà* has good cards but sees they hold a **"bare king"** (*rei pelat*) that may
fall, they may say **"es primer rei aida"** — *the first king helps* — so that the other
players put their king on them as soon as possible.

### 4.4 Following suit, and trumping

> Quan es qui és mà tira un pal, tots es altres han de tirar des mateix pal. Si no en tens,
> pots «fallar», si vols. Després has de tirar un altre pal fins que isca o posen rei. Si a
> un jugador li venen es quatre reis, s'acaba sa mà.

**Translation.** When the *mà* leads a suit, everyone else **must play that same suit**. If
you have none, **you may trump (*fallar*) if you want** — it is optional. **Afterwards you
must lead another suit until a king comes out or is put.** **If one player is dealt all
four kings, the hand ends.**

> **[uncertain]** The middle sentence, *"Després has de tirar un altre pal fins que isca o
> posen rei"*, most plausibly means the leader must keep leading a **different suit each
> basa** until the partnership is revealed, i.e. must go hunting for the king. It could
> also be read as a restatement of the discard option when void. Confirm before
> implementing.

### 4.5 Obligation to trump when a trump is led

> Si un trumfa d'espadilla, és obligatori tirar trumfo si en tens.
>
> Si un trumfa de manilla, és obligatori tirar trumfo; però es qui té l'espadilla se la
> juga si vol.
>
> Si un trumfa de basto és obligatori tirar trumfo tots. Es qui tenen espadilla i manilla,
> se la poden jugar si volen.
>
> Si un trumfa d'un altre trumfo que no siga l'espadilla, la manilla i el basto, tots han
> de tirar. Es qui tinguen ses tres cartes (espadilla, manilla, basto) se les poden jugar
> si volen.

**Translation.**

- If someone trumps with the **espadilla**, it is **obligatory to play a trump if you have
  one** — no exceptions.
- If someone trumps with the **manilla**, it is obligatory to play a trump; **but whoever
  holds the espadilla plays it only if they want to.**
- If someone trumps with the **basto**, everyone is obliged to play a trump. **Those
  holding the espadilla or the manilla play them only if they want to.**
- If someone trumps with **any other trump** that is not the espadilla, the manilla or the
  basto, everyone must play. **Those holding the three cards (espadilla, manilla, basto)
  play them only if they want to.**

> **The unified rule behind the four cases:** when a trump is led, you must play a trump if
> you hold one — except that you may **withhold a special card (espadilla, manilla, basto)
> that outranks the trump that was led.** Every exemption in the four sentences is exactly
> the set of cards higher than the card led.

### 4.6 Signalling with the horse

> Quan encara no hi ha rei es solen jugar es cavalls.
>
> Si es qui és mà juga un cavall i altre jugador no té aqueix pal (però té un rei i li pot
> aidar perquè té bones cartes), en lloc de fallar es jugarà una carta des mateix pal des
> seu rei i aixina li donarà a entendre que li'l posarà. En aquest cas, es qui és mà jugarà
> a continuació una carta des mateix pal des rei de qui li ha fet senyal.

**Translation.** While there is still no king out, players tend to lead **horses**.

If the *mà* plays a horse and another player is void in that suit — but holds a king and
can help, because they have good cards — then **instead of trumping they will play a card
of the same suit as their king**, giving the *mà* to understand that they will put it on
them. In that case the *mà* will next lead a card of the same suit as the king of the
player who signalled.

> This is a **convention between players, not an enforceable rule.** It constrains nothing
> the server can validate; it is a UI/affordance concern at most.

### 4.7 Fer primeres, and counting the basas

> Es qui van han de procurar «fer primeres», que consisteix en fer ses quatre primeres
> bases seguides. Aixina, si guanya, cobrarà més. Es contraris procuraran que això no
> ocórrega.
>
> Ses cartes que s'arrepleguen a cada basa es posen contraposades unes demunt de ses altres
> en forma de zig-zag perquè es puguen comptar millor.
>
> Un jugador es pot posar ell mateixa el rei, si veu que pot fer ses 5 bases.

**Translation.** The side that goes should try to ***fer primeres*** — to **win the first
four basas in a row**. That way, if they win, they collect more. The opponents will try to
stop it happening.

The cards gathered at each basa are laid crosswise on top of one another in a **zig-zag**
so they can be counted more easily.

**A player can put the king on themselves**, if they see they can make the 5 basas.

### 4.8 Fer todo, and the four kings

> «Fer todo» ho has de demanar quan tens cinc bases.
>
> Si a un li venen es quatre reis, cobra quatre, i s'acaba sa mà. Si en es mateix temps un
> altre jugador vullguera anar a soles, no podria. En aquest cas sols pot anar a soles es
> qui té es quatre reis.

**Translation.** ***Fer todo*** — making every basa — **you must call when you have five
basas.**

**If one player is dealt the four kings, they collect 4 and the hand ends.** If at the same
time another player wanted to go alone, they could not: in that case **only the holder of
the four kings may go alone.**

> Two consequences worth spelling out: the hand does **not** simply stop at five basas —
> at five the going side may call *todo* and play on for all eight; and a four-king deal
> **pre-empts a soledad declaration**.

## 5. Què es cobra? — What you collect

Amounts are collected **from the posso**. `cadegú` = "each" (per player of the side).

| Catalan | Translation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Si tens el dengue | You hold the dengue | **1** |
| Si tens l'estutxe i vas | You hold the estutxe and you go | **2** |
| Si et posen el rei i guanyes | The king is put on you and you win | **2** each |
| Per guanyar i fer primeres | Winning and making primeres | **3** each |
| Per guanyar i tindre l'estutxe | Winning and holding the estutxe | **3** each (**4** with the dengue) |
| Per guanyar, tindre l'estutxe i fer primeres | Winning, holding the estutxe and making primeres | **4** each (**5** if one of the going pair had the dengue) |
| Per «fer todo» | Making todo | **1** |
| Per tindre es quatre reis | Holding the four kings | **4** |
| Si et poses el rei i guanyes | You put the king on yourself and win | **4** |
| Si et poses el rei i guanyes i primeres | …and make primeres | **5** |
| Si vas a soles i guanyes | You go alone and win | **5** |
| Si vas a soles, guanyes i primeres | …and make primeres | **6** |
| Si vas a soles, guanyes, primeres i dengue | …with the dengue | **7** |
| Si vas a soles, guanyes, primeres, dengue i estutxe | …with the estutxe too | **8** |
| Si vas a soles, guanyes, primeres, dengue, estutxe i todo | …and todo | **9** |
| Si vas a soles, dengue, estutxe i todo | Alone, dengue, estutxe and todo | **9** |
| Si tens es 4 reis, primeres, estutxe i todo | Four kings, primeres, estutxe and todo | **13** |
| Si es que van fan menys de cinc bases | The going side makes fewer than five basas | **1** |
| Si es que va a soles paga | The lone player pays | **1** |

> És el màxim que es pot cobrar: 5 d'anar a soles, 4 des 4 reis, 1 de primeres, 2 de
> l'estutxe i 1 de todo.
>
> En cobres 1 si es qui van fan menys de 4 bases. En cobres 1 si es qui va a soles paga; si,
> a més, fera menys de quatre bases, en cobraries 2.

**Translation.** That is the maximum that can be collected: **5 for going alone, 4 for the
four kings, 1 for primeres, 2 for the estutxe and 1 for todo** — 13 in total.

You collect 1 [more] if the going side makes fewer than 4 basas. You collect 1 if the lone
player pays; if on top of that they made fewer than four basas, you would collect 2.

### 5.1 The additive model behind the table

Every row in the table above is the sum of one **base** and a set of **+1 increments**:

| Base — mutually exclusive | Amount |
|---|---|
| You go and are helped (the king was put on you), each of the pair | 2 |
| You put the king on yourself | 4 |
| You go alone (*a soles*) | 5 |
| You were dealt the four kings | 4 |

| Increment | Amount |
|---|---|
| *Primeres* — the first four basas in a row | +1 |
| *Dengue* — espadilla + basto | +1 |
| *Estutxe* — espadilla + manilla + basto | +1 **on top of** the dengue (so 2 together) |
| *Todo* — all eight basas, called at five | +1 |

The model reproduces every printed row, including the two that look inconsistent:

- `Si tens l'estutxe i vas, 2` = dengue 1 + estutxe 1. The estutxe contains the dengue, so
  holding it always scores both.
- `Si vas a soles, dengue, estutxe i todo, 9` = 5 + primeres 1 + dengue 1 + estutxe 1 +
  todo 1. **Todo implies primeres**, which is why primeres is not listed separately.
- `Si tens es 4 reis, primeres, estutxe i todo, 13` = 5 + 4 + 1 + 2 + 1, matching the
  author's own arithmetic in the "màxim" sentence. Going alone is implied, since with four
  kings only that player may go alone.

**Losing side, opposite the going side:** each opponent collects **1** when the going side
(or the lone player) fails, and **2** if they were held to fewer than four basas.

## 6. Què es paga? — What you pay

Amounts are paid **into the posso**.

| Catalan | Translation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Si et cau el rei | Your king falls (you were forced to play it) | **1** |
| Si t'aiden i perds | You are helped and you lose | **2** each |
| Si perden i primeres | They lose, with primeres | **3** each |
| Si perden i tenen l'estutxe | They lose and hold the estutxe | **3** each |
| Si perden primeres i estutxe | They lose, with primeres and the estutxe | **4** each |
| Si et poses el rei perds | You put the king on yourself and lose | **4** |
| Si et poses el rei i primeres | …with primeres | **5** |
| Si vas a soles i perds | You go alone and lose | **5** |

> El dengue sempre es cobra.

**Translation.** **The dengue is always collected** — never paid. Holding the espadilla and
the basto is worth 1 whether you win or lose.

The payment ladder mirrors the collection ladder exactly, base for base and increment for
increment, minus the dengue.

> **[uncertain]** In `Si perden i primeres` and `Si perden i tenen l'estutxe`, the source
> does not say **whose** primeres or estutxe raises the payment. The reading that makes the
> whole table self-consistent is that the **going side's own** claims scale their liability
> both ways — you stake more when you hold the estutxe or make primeres, and you pay more
> if you still lose. `tenen l'estutxe` ("*they* hold the estutxe", of the losers) supports
> it. Confirm before implementing.

| **Es primer rei aida** | Quan es qui va té bones cartes i té «un rei pelat» diu «Es primer rei aida», això és un reclam perquè li posen el rei com més prompte millor. | When the one who goes has good cards and a bare king, they say "the first king helps" — a call for the king to be put on them as soon as possible. |

| **Fallar** | Quan juguen d'un pal que tu no en tens, pots jugar un trumfo i fer-la. Això és fallar. | When a suit you are void in is led, you may play a trump and take the trick. That is *fallar*. |
| **Fer primeres** | Fer ses quatre primeres bases seguides. | To make the first four basas in a row. |
| **Fer senyal** | Donar a entendre an es qui va que tu vols posar el rei. | To let the one who goes understand that you want to put the king. |
| **Fer todo** | Fer totes ses bases. | To make every basa. |
| **Fer trumfos** | Dir es pal que són trumfos. | To name the trump suit. |

| **Posar el rei** | Voler aidar an es qui va. | To want to help the one who goes. |
| **Posso** | Depòsit que es posa enmig de sa taula. Pot ser de diners (unes monedes de poc valor), però també s'havia jugat amb altres coses (com ara, cigrons, metletes). | The deposit placed in the middle of the table. It can be money (small-value coins), but it has also been played with other things — chickpeas, almonds. |
| **Rovell** | As d'oros. | Ace of coins. |
| **Rei pelat** | Tindre un rei que no va acompanyat d'una carta més des seu pal. | Holding a king with no other card of its suit to go with it. |
| **Ser mà** | És es qui fa trumfos. És es primer en jugar. | The one who makes trumps. The first to play. |

> **[uncertain]** `Ser mà` defines the *mà* as both the trump-maker **and** the first to
> play, while §4.1 says a lone player makes trumps *"encara que no siga mà"* — even if they
> are not *mà*. The two statements pull apart precisely in a soledad round: the lone player
> names trumps, but the glossary keeps "first to play" attached to the *mà*. The reading
> that satisfies both sentences is that **in a soledad the lone player names trumps and the
> *mà* still leads**, which is what `spec.md` §2.5 already says. Confirm.

## 8. Notes finals — Closing note

> Aquest joc té moltíssimes jugades i combinacions, tantes que seria impossible de comptar.
> És complicat, i només es pot ensenyar jugant i practicant. Moltes coses deuen faltar a
> dir, però ja tenim una idea, encara que no siga perfecta, d'aquest joc tan entretingut i
> que mos fa passar-la tan rebé tantes vesprades.

**Translation.** This game has a great many plays and combinations — so many it would be
impossible to count them. It is complicated, and can only be taught by playing and
practising. Many things must be left unsaid, but we now have an idea, imperfect as it may
be, of this game that is such good entertainment and has given us so many fine evenings.

## Appendix: what the source does **not** cover

Do not read silence here as agreement with `spec.md`.

- The order of cards **within a non-trump suit** — only the trump-suit order is given.
- The **direction of play** around the table, and how the *mà* passes between hands.
- Whether the espadilla and basto may be played **out of suit** when you could follow.
  (Settled by decision, not by the source — see D-7 in `rules-diff.md`: they may not.)
- Who **leads the next basa** after one is won.
- The **size** of the posso stake, beyond "agreed in common, in equal parts".
- Any notion of a **draw**, an overall **game winner**, or a per-player coin balance.
