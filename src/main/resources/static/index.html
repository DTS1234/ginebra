Card order

Showing the order when is trump

Rules in one screen

  • Espadilla (A ⚔️ Espadas) is always the highest card, and Basto (A 🌳 Bastos) is always third. Neither belongs to its own suit for following, and either may be played at any time.
  • Manilla is always second and depends on trump: the 7 when Copas or Oros is trump, the 2 when Espadas or Bastos is trump.
  • Follow the suit that was led if you can. If a special card led, the suit to follow is trump. You are never forced to beat the cards already played.
  • Trump beats every non-trump card. A non-trump card can only win if nobody played trump and it is the highest of the led suit.
  • The low cards run in opposite directions by suit: in Copas and Oros the 2 is the best number and the 7 the worst; in Espadas and Bastos the 7 is the best and the 2 the worst. That holds whether or not the suit is trump.
  • The winner of a basa leads the next one.
  • A round ends the moment one team's basas add up to five - 5-0, 4-1, 3-2, however they are split. Eight basas at 4-4 is a draw.
