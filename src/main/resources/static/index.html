Card order

Showing the order when is trump

Rules in one screen

  • Espadilla (1 of Espadas) is always the highest card, and Basto (1 of Bastos) is always third. Neither belongs to its own suit for following, and either may be played at any time.
  • Manilla is always second and depends on trump: the 7 when Copas or Oros is trump, the 2 when Espadas or Bastos is trump.
  • Follow the suit that was led if you can. If a special card led, the suit to follow is trump. You are never forced to beat the cards already played.
  • Trump beats every non-trump card. A non-trump card can only win if nobody played trump and it is the highest of the led suit.
  • The low cards run in opposite directions by suit: in Copas and Oros the 2 is the best number and the 7 the worst; in Espadas and Bastos the 7 is the best and the 2 the worst. That holds whether or not the suit is trump.
  • The 1 changes place: above the King in its own suit when that suit is trump, below the Sota when it is not. Only Oros (rovell) and Copas (carabassa) have one — the other two are the specials.
  • The winner of a basa leads the next one.
  • Until a King comes out, whoever leads must open a suit that has not been led yet this hand.
  • The side that goes needs five basas. The other side blocks the moment it has four. There is no draw.

Your name

The name shows on your seat, so the other four know whose turn it is. Anything goes — it is only a label.

Lobby

Five players are needed. One creates a room, the other four join it from the list; the game starts the moment the fifth player is in. Short of people, take the empty seats with bots — they play a legal card at random, which is enough to see a hand through.
